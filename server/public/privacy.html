FIELDS
Travel Journal & Scrapbook · Privacy Policy
Effective Date: September 2026

1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy

FIELDS is built with privacy as a foundational principle. We believe personal travel memories belong to you. We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal profile creation. Your journal entries and generated posters are stored locally on your device.

2. Photo Processing & Artificial Intelligence

When you choose to create a field note, your selected photo is securely transmitted to our backend server for the sole purpose of generating your 4:3 rubber stamp poster and extracting sensory memory keywords.

  • No Training: Your uploaded photos and personal imagery are never used to train machine learning models.
  • Immediate Deletion: Uploaded photos are processed in memory and immediately discarded. We do not store, archive, or retain your original photos on our servers.
  • AI Providers: Processing is performed using privacy-compliant APIs from Google and xAI under enterprise terms that prohibit user data retention.

3. Device Identifiers & Anti-Abuse

To enforce our free usage allowance (2 free starter notes and 1 ad-supported note) without requiring user accounts or logins, the app generates a randomized, anonymous installation identifier and reads a non-personal device identifier. This is used strictly to prevent automated service abuse and manage purchased note credit balances.

4. Location & Metadata

FIELDS reads camera and photo capture metadata (EXIF GPS coordinates and capture timestamps) directly on your device to automatically detect travel locations (e.g. city and region) and the year taken. Location data is never used to track your physical movement or sold to any third party.

5. In-App Purchases & Advertising

  • In-App Purchases: Payments for note packs are processed directly through Apple App Store or Google Play Store via RevenueCat. We never have access to your credit card or billing details.
  • Advertising: Rewarded video ads are provided via Google AdMob to unlock your 3rd field note. Ad requests are non-personalized by default.

6. Data Deletion & Contact

You have complete control over your data. You can delete all locally saved notes, posters, and preferences at any time directly within the app under Settings → Reset Notebook.

For questions or privacy inquiries, contact us at: support@fieldnotes.app
