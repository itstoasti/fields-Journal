◈ Three Simple Steps

How your journey becomes a collectible.

STEP 01

Choose Any Photo

Snap on the go or pick any photo from your camera roll. Your destination and capture year are automatically detected and stamped into your card header.

58% Photo Area Auto-Location Stamping
STEP 02

Atmospheric Memory

We distill the exact emotional feeling of your scene into three poetic memory words (e.g. Cedar trees · Temple bell · Dusk mist). You can tap to change or customize them.

Poetic Memory Triad Customizable Words
STEP 03

The Hand-Pressed Stamp

Your photo is minted into a bespoke multi-color rubber stamp print with relief carving notches, dry ink texture, and tactile misregistration pressed onto warm aged parchment.
